# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The TaranMidwife community aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone.

We welcome participation regardless of experience, background, identity, or technical expertise.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Welcome different perspectives.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Communicate responsibly, especially when discussing healthcare and patient safety.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption or intimidation.
- Misleading claims about project capabilities or clinical approval.
- Sharing real patient data, credentials, or confidential information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this policy.

Concerns may be reported privately to the project owner through the contact information published by PT Taran Rainmaker Group.

This policy applies to project spaces and community interactions related to TaranMidwife.
